Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026

Lux Vault respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use our website or place an order and how that information may be used to process and fulfil your order.

1. Information We Collect

To process and deliver orders, we may collect information such as:

  • Customer name
  • Phone number
  • Delivery address
  • Order and product details
  • Payment-related information required to process an order
  • Information provided when contacting customer support

2. How We Use Your Information

Customer information may be used for purposes including:

  • Confirming orders
  • Processing orders
  • Arranging product delivery
  • Providing order-related communication
  • Handling replacement requests
  • Providing customer support
  • Preventing misuse or fraudulent activity
  • Maintaining and improving the operation of the website

3. Order Fulfillment & Service Providers

To fulfil an order, relevant customer information may need to be shared with delivery, fulfillment, payment or other service providers involved in completing the order.

Information is shared only to the extent reasonably necessary for the relevant service or order-related purpose.

4. Payment Information

Payment-related information may be processed through the applicable payment process used for the order.

Lux Vault does not intentionally request or store sensitive payment credentials such as UPI PINs, card PINs or passwords.

5. Cash on Delivery Orders

For COD orders, customer contact and delivery information may be used to confirm and fulfil the order and coordinate delivery.

6. Marketing

Customer order information is primarily used for order processing, delivery, customer support and related operational purposes.

We do not sell customer personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with relevant service providers when reasonably necessary to process, deliver, support or manage an order.

8. Data Security

Lux Vault takes reasonable steps to protect information provided by customers.

However, no internet transmission, electronic storage system or online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

Customer information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for order processing, customer support, replacement handling, business records, security, dispute resolution or applicable legal requirements.

10. Third-Party Links

The Lux Vault website may contain links to third-party websites or services. Lux Vault is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.

Customers should review the privacy policies of third-party services before providing information to them.

11. Children's Information

Lux Vault is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. Customers should not provide personal information belonging to another person without appropriate authorization.

12. Your Information

If you have questions about information associated with your order, you may contact Lux Vault using the contact details below.
