Return & Replacement Policy

Last Updated: 2026

Important: Lux Vault does not provide refunds for eligible product issues. Eligible wrong or defective products may be replaced subject to the conditions below.

1. Replacement Period

If you receive a product that is eligible for replacement, you must contact Lux Vault within 24 hours of delivery.

Requests submitted after this 24-hour period may not be eligible for replacement.

2. Eligible Replacement Cases

Replacement may be considered only when the product received is:

  • Incorrectly delivered; or
  • Defective at the time of delivery.

The product must be returned in the condition required for verification and replacement processing.

3. No Refund Policy

Lux Vault does not offer refunds for products that are eligible for replacement.

Where a valid replacement case is approved, the applicable solution will be replacement of the eligible product rather than a cash or UPI refund.

4. Unboxing Video Requirement

For wrong, damaged or defective product claims, Lux Vault may require a clear unboxing video showing the package and product condition.

The unboxing video must be recorded continuously and must be uncut, unedited and without retakes.

The video should clearly show the package, opening process and the condition of the product received.

5. Information Required

When contacting Lux Vault regarding a replacement request, customers may be required to provide:

  • Order number
  • Detailed description of the issue
  • Clear photographs of the product and packaging
  • Required unboxing video

6. Electronics

Electronic products are not covered under this replacement policy.

7. Non-Eligible Cases

Replacement will generally not be available for:

  • Requests submitted after the 24-hour reporting period.
  • Damage caused by misuse or improper handling.
  • Accidental damage caused after delivery.
  • Normal wear and tear.
  • Damage caused by the customer.
  • Change of mind or personal preference.
  • Cases where required verification cannot be provided.
  • Electronic products.

8. Verification

All replacement requests are subject to verification and approval. Submission of a request does not automatically guarantee replacement.

Additional photographs, videos or other information may be requested when necessary to verify the issue.

9. Product Return

If a replacement request is approved, the customer may be required to return the product using the return instructions provided by Lux Vault.

The return process must be completed according to the instructions provided for the specific approved case.

10. Replacement Processing

Once the issue has been verified and the required return process, where applicable, has been completed, the replacement will be processed according to product availability and fulfillment requirements.

Replacement delivery may take additional processing time.

11. Replacement Shipping

For an approved eligible replacement case, applicable replacement shipping arrangements will be handled according to the replacement process.

12. Customer Responsibility

  • Inspect the package and product immediately after delivery.
  • Report an eligible issue within 24 hours.
  • Keep the original packaging until the issue is resolved.
  • Provide clear photographs and video when requested.
  • Follow the return instructions provided by Lux Vault.

13. Policy Changes

Lux Vault may update or modify this Return & Replacement Policy when necessary. The latest version will be published on this website.
