Return & Replacement Policy

Last Updated: 2026

1. Replacement Period

Lux Vault provides a replacement option for eligible products within 7 days of delivery.

2. Wrong or Damaged Products

If you receive a wrong or damaged product, please contact Lux Vault as soon as possible.

Important: A clear video showing the package opening and the condition of the product may be required for verification.

If the wrong or damaged product is verified based on the required evidence, Lux Vault will bear the applicable return or replacement delivery cost.

3. Customer-Requested Returns

If a return is requested for reasons other than receiving a wrong or damaged product, applicable delivery or return charges may be borne by the customer.

A ₹50 delivery charge may apply where applicable.

4. Product Condition

Products requested for replacement should be returned in the condition required for verification and replacement.

5. Verification

Lux Vault may review the submitted video, photographs and order information before approving a replacement request.

6. Refunds

Replacement is the primary resolution for eligible wrong or damaged products. Where a refund is approved, it will be processed through an available UPI payment method.
