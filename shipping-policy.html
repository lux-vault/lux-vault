Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last Updated: 2026

1. Delivery Locations

Lux Vault currently provides delivery services across India.

2. Order Processing

Orders are generally processed within 1–3 business days after payment confirmation.

Orders placed on weekends or public holidays may be processed on the next available business day.

3. Delivery Time

Standard delivery is generally expected within 5–7 business days from the shipment date.

The total time from order placement to delivery may therefore include both the order-processing period and the shipping period.

Delivery times may vary depending on the delivery location, courier availability, weather, transportation conditions and other circumstances outside our reasonable control.

4. Shipping & Delivery Charges

Applicable delivery charges are shown during checkout before the order is confirmed.

For UPI orders, Lux Vault currently applies:

  • Orders below ₹499: ₹50 delivery charge
  • Orders of ₹499 or above: Free delivery

For COD orders, an applicable ₹50 COD delivery charge is currently applied.

5. Order Confirmation

After an order is successfully placed, the relevant order information may be used to process and fulfil the order.

Shipping or tracking information may be provided when available.

6. Tracking

Where tracking information is available, it may be shared with the customer through the available communication channel.

7. Delivery Address

Customers are responsible for providing a complete and accurate delivery address, phone number and other required delivery details.

Lux Vault is not responsible for delivery problems caused by incorrect, incomplete or outdated information supplied by the customer.

8. Delivery Delays

Although we aim to follow the estimated delivery timeline, unexpected courier, weather, transportation, operational or other circumstances may cause delays.

Estimated delivery times are not guaranteed delivery deadlines.

9. Delivery Issues

Customers should inspect the package and product promptly after delivery.

If you receive a wrong or defective product, contact Lux Vault within 24 hours of delivery and follow the Return & Replacement Policy.

An uncut and unedited unboxing video may be required for verification.

10. Customer Responsibility

  • Provide accurate delivery details.
  • Remain available to receive the order.
  • Check the package and product after delivery.
  • Report eligible delivery/product issues within 24 hours.
  • Follow replacement verification requirements.

11. Policy Changes

Lux Vault may update or modify this Shipping & Delivery Policy when necessary. The latest version will be published on this website.
