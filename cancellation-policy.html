Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: 2026

Important: Orders cannot be cancelled once they have been successfully placed.

1. Order Cancellation

Once an order has been successfully placed, the customer cannot cancel the order through Lux Vault.

This applies to both prepaid UPI orders and confirmed Cash on Delivery (COD) orders.

2. Before Completing an Order

Customers should carefully check the product, quantity, name, phone number, delivery address, payment method and total amount before completing the order.

3. After Shipping

Orders cannot be cancelled after they have been shipped.

4. Incorrect Customer Information

Customers are responsible for providing accurate and complete delivery information.

Lux Vault is not responsible for delivery issues caused by incorrect, incomplete or outdated customer information.

5. Refused or Unsuccessful Delivery

Refusing delivery, remaining unavailable for delivery or providing incorrect delivery information does not constitute an approved cancellation.

Any applicable consequences arising from an unsuccessful delivery may depend on the order and fulfillment process.

6. Wrong or Defective Products

If you receive a wrong or defective product, this is not treated as an order cancellation.

Such cases are handled under the Return & Replacement Policy.

Eligible issues must be reported within 24 hours of delivery.

7. Refunds

Cancellation does not create a right to a refund because orders cannot be cancelled once successfully placed.

Lux Vault does not provide refunds under its replacement process. Eligible wrong or defective products may be handled through replacement according to the Return & Replacement Policy.
